
# What is Communication Craft?

**Communication Craft is the communication part of Workcraft.** It concerns how people and AI create, receive, assess and improve communication so that the work becomes understandable to those involved.

[Workcraft](../core/what-is-workcraft.md) is the wider framework for automating work through AI. Communication Craft develops this part of that framework: what a contribution needs to convey, how to express it, how to understand what arrives, and how to recognize and repair meaningful problems.

**Communication Craft has two connected parts: theory and methodology, and implementable skills.** Together, they provide the understanding and practical means for applying Communication Craft to AI to automate communication work.

## The two parts of Communication Craft

### 1. Theory and methodology

This part develops our understanding of communication and how to work with it. The theory explains the relevant concepts, relationships and qualities. The methodology explains how to use that understanding when creating, receiving, assessing and improving a contribution.

The findings collected here contribute to this foundation: the exchange and its purpose, the meaning to be conveyed, the working essence, materialization, quality checks and diagnostic revision.

### 2. Implementable skills

This part turns the theory and methodology into skills that AI can execute. A skill gives concrete instructions for carrying out a communication task, including the inputs it needs, the work to perform and the result to produce or inspect.

The purpose is to make Communication Craft applicable in practice, so AI can automate communication work. Explicitness detection and repair are existing examples of this direction; the broader foundation supports further skills for creating, receiving, assessing and revising communication.

The two parts belong together. The theory and methodology guide what the skills should do and why. Applying the skills provides evidence about where that understanding or its implementation needs improvement.

## Why this is needed

A message can be fluent and still fail its purpose. It can omit an important reason, overstate what has been achieved, leave a relationship unclear, or give a technical explanation when the exchange needs an operational account.

One of the examples behind this work makes the problem concrete. A longer explanation gave two reasons for declining a proposed restriction. A later short message retained one reason and lost the other. The shorter message changed the account of the decision's basis. Improving individual phrases would not necessarily recover the missing reason.

The receiving side matters too. A person or AI needs to understand what a contribution actually says, what it establishes, what it leaves unanswered and where interpretation remains uncertain. That understanding can be useful even when no reply or feedback is sent.

Communication Craft is needed to make these concerns explicit enough to work with. It should help us explain what is wrong, what grounds that judgment, what a better contribution would supply, and how to check whether a revision has achieved it.

## What we are trying to make possible

The work has several connected aims:

- **Create a suitable contribution.** Establish what the exchange needs, then express the relevant meaning in a form the recipient can use.
- **Understand an incoming contribution.** Interpret what was communicated using the available context, preserving consequential uncertainty.
- **Assess communication on stated grounds.** Use concrete questions and evidence to judge qualities such as explicitness, faithful meaning and fit to the exchange.
- **Repair the actual problem.** Distinguish an expression problem from missing understanding, unsupported content or underlying work that has not been completed.
- **Preserve meaning through change.** Keep necessary reasons, relationships, qualifications and intended openness when shortening, reorganizing or answering a contribution.

The aim is to make these activities dependable enough to support automation. A useful check therefore needs more than a quality label: we need to know when it applies, what it examines and what evidence supports its judgment.

## The understanding established so far

### Start with the particular exchange

Good communication depends on what is happening between its participants. Who is speaking, to whom, for what purpose, with what relevant context and expectations? What does the recipient need to understand from this contribution?

The same subject can need different explanations in different exchanges. A technical explanation, an operational update and a request for a decision can draw on the same work while answering different questions. These views can combine. Their usefulness follows from the exchange rather than from a fixed label for the recipient.

### Establish the meaning that needs to be conveyed

The recent findings use **communication substrate** and **working essence** to make the basis for communication explicit. The current account connects the situation of the exchange with the particular contribution being intended or understood.

Knowing that a message is “an explanation to a reviewer” is useful orientation. We still need the actual reasons, claims and qualifications. Their relationships matter: a reason explains a particular choice; evidence supports a particular claim; a qualification limits what is being asserted.

This working understanding stays accountable to the task and available support. It can be revised when new information or reasoning shows that it is incomplete or wrong.

### Express, inspect and revise

The working account can be expressed in different ways. The findings call an actual expression a **materialization**. Two messages can differ in wording and arrangement while preserving the meaning needed for their use.

The emerging creation process is:

**Establish the working essence → express it → review the actual contribution → determine what needs to change → revise and inspect the result.**

A review may reveal that the text lost a meaning already present in the essence. It may instead reveal that the essence itself is incomplete. Sometimes the missing answer requires more information or substantive work. Those cases need different responses.

Explicitness is one established focus within this wider work. The detector and fixer work has helped make particular wording gaps observable and repairable. Broader meaning and fidelity judgments also need the task and supporting basis. Agreement between a draft and its essence cannot establish adequacy if both omit the same important point.

### Receive and reuse understanding

Receiving builds a supported interpretation from the communication and available context. It approximates what the sender conveyed and intended; it does not give direct access to the sender's private understanding.

Deliberate quality assessment is optional. A later reply can reuse the received context while establishing its own purpose, stance and meaning. Understanding a request does not, by itself, decide whether to accept it, decline it or ask for clarification.

## How it belongs within Workcraft

Communication Craft works with the rest of Workcraft. Relevant information has to be available; missing reasoning may have to be developed; intended and actual results need comparison; the next action has to be chosen and coordinated.

The communication responsibility is to make the contribution understandable and assessable within that work. A clearer message can explain an unfinished task accurately. It cannot turn the unfinished task into a completed one.

This is why Communication Craft can be developed as a focused part of Workcraft while retaining its connections to context, reasoning, alignment and orchestration.

## Where the work stands

Both parts are in development. The theory and methodology now include the conceptual foundation and proposed communication loop. Existing communication skills, including explicitness detection and repair, provide concrete implementations of parts of the work. The full proposed loop has not yet been established as a tested implementation.

Some questions remain open: the best representation for a working essence, reliable selection of necessary meaning, how checks should be combined, and how much the complete process improves real communication at an acceptable cost. The findings do not establish a final taxonomy or a universally required format.

This folder brings that work together. It provides an explanation of the purpose and a record of the findings behind the current understanding. Earlier findings retain their original proposals and qualifications, including where later work changed direction.
