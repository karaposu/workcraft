

# Group trip messages: answer key

Patterns are those in `non-ambiguity-patterns.md` (P01 to P32). Each planted ambiguity is a real problem for the reader named in the setting under that pattern's "Problem when" clause, and none of its "Not a problem when" conditions holds. Where a planted gap could reasonably be labelled under a neighbouring pattern, the entry says so under "Also acceptable"; score the gap, not the label.

Types: 22 seeded, 4 clean (T05, T13, T21, T28), 4 decoys (T03, T10, T17, T25).

## T01 — seeded

**A001 — P04**
- Words: "the Blue Door"
- Sentence: "From the station, head straight to the Blue Door and we'll sort out the rest when you get there."
- Reader cannot establish: what or where the Blue Door is. Leo was never in the planning chat, and nothing introduces it. It could be a café, the apartment's entrance, a bar or a landmark.
- Example clarification: "head straight to the Blue Door, the café opposite our apartment at Rua das Flores 30".

**A002 — P11** (same sentence as A001)
- Words: "sort out the rest"
- Reader cannot establish: what will happen on arrival and what "the rest" covers: handing over keys, paying, explaining the plan for the weekend, or something else. He cannot tell what to have ready.
- Also acceptable: P01 for "the rest".
- Example clarification: "and I'll give you your key and the weekend plan when you get there".

**A003 — P25**
- Words: "your share of the deposit in cash, as agreed"
- Sentence: "Bring your share of the deposit in cash, as agreed."
- Reader cannot establish: what the deposit is for and how much his share is. "As agreed" points to an agreement made in a chat Leo was not part of. Readings: a deposit on the apartment, a deposit for a booking such as a tour, or a damage deposit; the amount is unknown.
- Also acceptable: P01 for "the deposit".
- Example clarification: "Bring €40 in cash, your share of the €240 damage deposit the apartment owner wants on arrival."
