# Non-ambiguous communication

## Why this skill exists

Communication lets a reader understand another person's account of the work. That understanding depends on knowing what the words refer to, how the things being discussed relate, and why the present point matters.

When an AI omits that meaning, the user must recover it through questions such as “which project?”, “a row of what?”, “start doing what?” or “why are we discussing this?”. Recovering the meaning becomes extra work before they can use the answer. This is the communication friction the skill addresses.

## The central problem

The agreed problem definition is:

> AI writes as though I share its detailed working context. I usually remember the direction, but I may have forgotten the objects, relationships, decisions and history behind its explanation.
>
> Every answer therefore needs to restore the prerequisite context for that answer: identifiable, searchable names; what those things represent; how they relate; and why the present point matters. I should be able to understand and direct the work without reconstructing earlier conversations.

The user moves between several pieces of work. They may recognise a name without remembering its role or the discussion that made it relevant. Write with that difference between the AI's working context and the reader's remembered context in mind.

## What non-ambiguity means here

An explanation is non-ambiguous when the reader can establish its intended meaning from the explanation and the background it reasonably assumes.

The skill addresses both wording that allows different interpretations and omitted context that leaves the intended meaning inaccessible. Naming a table, for example, may identify its location while still leaving unclear what its records represent.

The explanation should supply the context needed for the particular point being made. A short answer may need one explicit name or relationship. A longer explanation may need the relevant history and the connections between its points. Its length and structure should follow that need.

## Mandatory reference

**Before drafting or revising with this skill, read [Non-ambiguity principles](references/non-ambiguity-principles.md) in full.** This is the skill's required reference. If its current contents have already been read in full and remain available in the active context, use them.

The reference contains the principles, explanatory examples and review questions for making the intended meaning accessible to the reader. It covers identifying referents, explaining relationships, making actions and purposes concrete, restoring prerequisite context, and preserving meaning across an explanation and its revisions.

Maintain the principles in that separate file. This entry point explains the skill's purpose and directs the AI to the guidance it must apply.

## Using the skill

Establish the question being answered and the text being written or revised. Read the required reference, then apply it using the available context. Before returning the answer, use the reference's review questions to check whether the reader would still need an earlier conversation to understand the intended meaning.

Return the explanation in the form requested. The skill does not prescribe a fixed report layout; the reference governs how the words and their connections carry the meaning.
